# Matrix TOP Chip Wrapper And Pad Contract

Status: architecture contract before per-block physical implementation. This is
not a final pad-ring netlist, not a routed top assembly, and not signoff.

## Active Digital Core

`TOP/rtl/spadmic_top_matrix_v1.sv` is the active digital matrix-top core. It is
not the final pad-ring wrapper. The final chip wrapper must instantiate this
core, the PLL macro, the matrix macro, the SLVS/DDR output drivers, pads, and
the custom BOX_RING/pad-ring assembly.

Protected files remain protected:

- no edits to MPTDC internals;
- no edits to legacy `TOP/rtl/spadmic_top_v1.sv`;
- no silent replacement of the matrix-top core with a full-chip wrapper.

## Physical Layout Sources

The top physical assembly is custom and already constrained by layout:

- BOX_RING/pad-ring source: `/group/validmgr/PROJET/Prj_xh018/ksabra/cds/design/SPADMIC`;
- current active generator die envelope: `4293.179 um x 3209.173 um`;
- layout-description normalized hint: about `X=3200 um`, `Y=3700 um`.

The coordinate-frame difference is intentional documentation, not a resolved
geometry change. Until a parseable BOX_RING export maps those frames, the
staged floorplan generator records both and gates geometry using explicit
script arguments.

## Clock And PLL Wrapper

External clock pads:

- one external 160 MHz clock pad only: `clk_160m_ext_i`;
- no independent external pads for `clk_cfg_40m` or `clk_ref_40m`.

Reset default must select the PLL 160 MHz source. CSR may later select the
external 160 MHz source. In external-clock mode, one divide-by-4 clock feeds
both logical 40 MHz domains.

The active digital core now exposes CSR-controlled PLL/wrapper outputs:

- `pll_fint_sel_o[7:0]`: SelA_Fint through SelH_Fint;
- `pll_ro_sw_o[4:0]`: Sw0_RO through Sw4_RO;
- `pll_sel_pulse_pfd_o`;
- `pll_enable_div_o`;
- `pll_sel_40m_o`;
- `clk_160m_ext_select_o`.

PLL lock is observed by the core through `pll_lock_i` and is CSR-visible only.

The following PLL macro controls are external pad inputs handled by the final
pad-ring wrapper, not generated by the digital core:

- `pll_Ibi_KVCO_i`;
- `pll_Icp_i`;
- `pll_Ref_in_pll_ro_i`;
- `pll_Rst_Div_i`;
- `pll_Rst_CP_i`.

PLL VDD/GND planning uses the digital VDD/VSS domain. AVDD/AVSS remain
matrix/analog macro-owned.

## SLVS/Receiver GPIO From CSR

The active digital core exposes CSR-driven internal outputs for the
SLVS/receiver/tap-monitor custom block shown in the chip-level schematic. These
signals are not direct external pads. The final top wrapper routes them from
the digital core to the custom analog/IO block.

Driver-side controls:

- `slvs_s_drv_o[3:0]`
- `slvs_en_vref_ext_o`
- `slvs_en_drv_o`
- `slvs_vref_adj_b_o`
- `slvs_en_vref_400mv_o`
- `slvs_en_ref_drv_b_o`

Receiver-side controls:

- `rx_s_rx_o[3:0]`
- `rx_en_rx_o`
- `rx_en_term_o`

They are controlled by `SPADMIC_CSR_SLVS_GPIO_CTRL = 16'h7010`. Reserved bits
read as zero. The `_B` controls are passed through without digital inversion;
analog active-low polarity and reset defaults remain to be confirmed.

## I2C Reset Pad

The active core adds `i2c_rst_i`, corresponding to pad name `i2c_RST`.
This reset is active high and resets only the I2C slave/CSR bridge transport.
It does not clear the full matrix-top CSR image and does not reset acquisition,
matrix configuration, MPTDC axes, FIFO, or DDR16 state by itself.

The global reset remains `async_rst_n`.

## Matrix Macro Boundary

Matrix signals remain internal macro connections, not external digital pads:

- `R/Y/B`;
- `Rz/Yz/Bz`;
- `Din/Cin/Dout/Cout`;
- matrix supplies and `VTUNE`.

The matrix macro stays left/center-left. Position frontend logic may be
distributed around matrix pin corridors, then consolidated toward the
cluster/packet region.

## MPTDC Placement Contract

The three MPTDC blocks remain physical macros/placeholders to the right of the
matrix:

- R on top, Y in the middle, B on the bottom;
- close together;
- same orientation where possible;
- full DEF/block boundary `1061.20 um x 801.92 um`;
- 5 percent dimensional planning margin;
- 20 um provisional halo;
- 20 um inter-axis gap for the current tight-stack planning case.

MPTDC internals remain untouched.

## DDRs2 And SLVS Contract

The digital output path is:

```text
event_bundle_tx -> output_fifo_256 -> ddr16_pairer
  -> spadmic_ddrs2_adapter
  -> DDRs2 analog/custom macro
```

The digital core presents:

- `ddr_data_l_o[15:0]`;
- `ddr_data_h_o[15:0]`;
- `ddr_pair_valid_o`;
- `ddr_clk_o`.

The adapter maps that internal DDR16 contract to DDRs2:

- `DATA_L<15:0>` / `DATA_H<15:0>`: data lanes;
- `DATA_L<16>` / `DATA_H<16>`: valid lane, both equal to `ddr_pair_valid_o`;
- `DATA_L<17>` / `DATA_H<17>`: forwarded-clock lane, default `0/1`;
- `DATA_L<18>` / `DATA_H<18>`: spare lane tied low;
- `CLK_160M`: forwarded from the selected 160 MHz source.

The old DDR8 RTL boundary is obsolete for final silicon.

## Calibration Pads

The six calibration inputs remain external top-level pads:

## Per-Block Physical Flow

Do not launch full-top Genus by default. The implementation flow is staged by
sub-block and must produce reviewable collateral before top assembly:

1. `matrix_reset_ctrl`
2. `or64_tree`
3. `position_snapshot`
4. `matrix_cfg_ctrl`
5. `event_coordinator`
6. `event_bundle_tx`
7. `output_fifo`
8. `matrix_top_csr`
9. `i2c_csr_bridge`
10. `i2c_slave`
11. `ddr16_pairer`
12. `ddrs2_adapter`

The target for each hardened or semi-hardened block is netlist, DEF, LEF or
abstract, and enough reports for Virtuoso/OA top assembly with the analog
designer. Timing constraints stay relaxed for early feasibility; DRC/LVS
readiness, obstruction handling, clean placement, and routability are higher
priority at this stage than final timing closure.
